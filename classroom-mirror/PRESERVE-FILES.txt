  PRESERVING GOOGLE DRIVE FILES BEFORE YOUR ACCOUNT CLOSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The classroom-snapshot.json captures all text content and links.
However, the actual files (student PDFs, videos, Drive documents, etc.)
live in Google Drive under the school account and will stop being
accessible once that account is closed.

Here are the best strategies to preserve them:


OPTION 1 — Google Takeout (Easiest, gets everything)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Google Takeout lets you download a complete copy of all your Google data.

  1. Sign into your school Google account
  2. Go to https://takeout.google.com
  3. Click "Deselect all", then scroll and tick:
       ✓ Classroom
       ✓ Drive
  4. Click "Next step" → choose export frequency: "Export once"
  5. File type: .zip, size: 50 GB (or whatever fits)
  6. Click "Create export"
  7. Google will email you when it's ready (can take hours for large accounts)
  8. Download the zip files and save them somewhere safe

The Classroom export includes all posts, assignments, and submissions as
structured data. The Drive export includes all the actual files.

⚠ Important: Do this BEFORE the account is closed. You cannot do a
Takeout after an account is suspended or deleted.


OPTION 2 — Transfer specific files to your personal Google account
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For important individual files:

  1. Open Google Drive in your school account
  2. Right-click the file → Share → Add your personal Gmail address
  3. In your personal account: open the shared file → "Make a copy"
     (this saves a copy to your personal Drive)

For bulk transfer:
  - Select multiple files in Drive → right-click → Share → add personal email
  - Then in your personal account, use "Shared with me" → select all → "Add to My Drive"

Note: "Add to My Drive" only adds a shortcut. You need "Make a copy" for
true ownership transfer. For many files, Takeout (Option 1) is easier.


OPTION 3 — Share Classroom folders with personal account
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Google Classroom automatically creates a folder in Drive for each class
under "Classroom" in your school Drive.

  1. In school Drive, find the "Classroom" folder
  2. Right-click it → Share → add your personal Gmail
  3. In your personal account, open the shared folder
  4. Select all → "Organise" → "Add shortcut to Drive" OR
     Download the entire folder to your computer

Note: Shared drives owned by the school may be inaccessible after the
account closes even if shared with you. Download locally to be safe.


OPTION 4 — For student submission files specifically
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Student submissions are often in:
  - "Classroom" folder → [Course Name] → [Assignment Name] → Student Work

You can access these via Google Drive and download them. However, student-
created files stay in their own Drive accounts — you'll only have the copies
that were "submitted" to you (turned in through Classroom).


EXTERNAL LINKS (Figma, GitHub, external websites, etc.)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Links to Figma, GitHub repos, YouTube videos, personal websites, etc. that
students submitted are NOT Google Drive files. These links will continue to
work as long as the student's account exists and the file is still public.

The classroom-snapshot.json preserves all these URLs so you can still see
and click every link in the web app. Whether those links remain active
depends on the external service and the student.


TIMELINE RECOMMENDATION
─────────────────────────
6 months before closure:
  → Run Google Takeout (Drive + Classroom)
  → Transfer important files to personal Drive

3 months before closure:
  → Create/update the classroom-snapshot.json
  → Verify the web app works in Archive mode
  → Back up snapshot.json to a second location (USB, cloud)

Day before closure:
  → Final Takeout run for any new content
  → Final snapshot update

After closure:
  → The web app works from the local snapshot
  → Drive links will stop working (use Takeout files instead)
  → External links (Figma, YouTube, etc.) may still work
